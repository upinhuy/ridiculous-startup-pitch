One sentence, at most 200 characters, pitching a bizarre startup idea that
sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell — an entry for the
[make.fun bounty "Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a).

## The pitch

> We bottle the air from the exact street where you grew up and sell it to homesick expats for $29 a jar — nostalgia is the only import no tariff can touch.

The odd thing being sold: jars of air bottled on the exact street you grew
up on. Who pays: homesick expats, $29 a jar. Why it holds together:
nostalgia is a real market — people already buy hometown food, music and
souvenirs to feel home again — and a sealed jar of the air from your own
street is the most literal version of that feeling.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`, with a guard that refuses
to write anything outside the gate (UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, not only
white space).

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
